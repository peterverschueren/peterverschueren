The Shop

About Bookzwang

Bookzwang is a small, personal shop selling secondhand chess books and the occasional chess item. Everything here comes from my own collection, built over many years of playing, studying, and hunting through second-hand shops.

The books are carefully described and fairly priced. Most are in good to very good condition. I ship worldwide, carefully packed, at cost.

There is no shopping cart, no instant checkout. You see something you like, you send me an email. We arrange the rest at a human pace.
